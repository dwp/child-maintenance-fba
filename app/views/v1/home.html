Single parent support hub

This support hub provides guidance and tools for parents navigating family separation.

Although we try to keep links up to date, we cannot be responsible for external websites. We do not collect any data while you are using this service and cannot help with any private arrangements.

Work out payments for your child

Using our tools, you can work out the cost of your child and calculate your child maintenance.

Plan your private arrangement

Learn how to have productive conversations and use our tool to build a written agreement to guide your contributions toward your child.

Use the Child Maintenance Service

If you need a government-managed child maintenane case, you can apply online.

Get advice on separation

Explore resources from recommended organisations. They can help you talk to your children about separation, find a mediator, and more.

Get financial advice

Government and recommend organisations can help you with benefits, budgets and debt.

Get additional support

Explore resources for single parents and mental health.
