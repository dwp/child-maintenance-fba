Create an arrangement agreement

Most children benefit from having both parents or guardians involved in their lives in a positive way, whether they live with them or not. Parents can help to do this by working together with their children’s long-term interests at heart.

This arrangement form can help you work together to make sure your children receive as much support as possible. Use it to make a record of what you have both agreed.

You can download or print the finished form off after filling it in.

You can work out how much each parent might need to pay first by using our calculate the cost of your child tool.
