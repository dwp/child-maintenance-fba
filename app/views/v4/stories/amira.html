Amira and Martin have 3 preteen children. After a long while of constant arguments, their relationship officially ended 2 years ago when they decided they did not want the children to grow up in a household of tension. Amira pushed to equally share costs.

Working out a financial arrangement

To Amira's relief, Martin moved back to his parents' house. However, money was tight for both and they still could not agree on a way forward for care or finances. Amira deided to write down everything she wanted to discuss and shared it with Martin in advance to avoid surprises.

He agreed to meet at a cafe, where they were more likely to stay calm. They started by talking about school costs -- uniforms and lunch. To keep it fair, they decided to each add lunch credit every other month and to split the cost of uniforms. Logically, they concldued, the same should apply to the kids' hobbies and clothes.

The arrangement now

Amira's dislike of Martin has cooled a bit since he's proven to be reliable in the long term. And, to her surprise, his new partner has made a great impression. She convinced Martin to contribute toward the food bill as the kids have gotten older.
