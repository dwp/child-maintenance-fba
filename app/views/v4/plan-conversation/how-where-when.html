Think about somewhere to meet where you will both feel able to talk to each other. Depending on where you feel comfortable, you could meet at:

  • one parent's home
  • a friend or family member's home
  • a public place (for example, a cafe or library)

If meeting face to face will not work, think about other forms of communication (for example, text, email or telephone).

Try to agree on a time when you can talk openly, without children around. Leave yourself plenty of time so you are not under pressure.
