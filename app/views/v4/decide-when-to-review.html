There are many valid reasons to review the financial and care arrangments for your child. You can {% if data['arrangementSaved'] == 'yes' %} review your arrangement (opens in a new tab) {% else %} review your arrangement (opens in a new tab) {% endif %} on your own or with the other parent.

There are two common ways to review your arrangement:

  • on a regular schedule
  • due to a change in your circumstances

Reviewing on a schedule

You can choose to review your arrangement with a certain frequency, for example once per year or every two years.

You can also choose to review on a certain date, for example on your child's birthday or the first day of school each year.

Reviewing for a change in your circumstances

Changes could include:

  • an update to your care arrangment
  • an increase or decrease in income
  • getting benefits
  • a new medical condition
  • a milestone for your child (for example, starting a new school)
  • moving far away or abroad
  • if your child has a new adoptive parent
  • if your child loses a step-parent
