The Child Maintenance Service is a government service. It can:

  • work out payment amounts based on care arrangements and the paying parent's income
  • manage payments from one parent to the other for you
  • take action to ensure payments are made on time and in full
  • help find the other parent if you have lost contact
  • keep your location and contact details private

Parents often choose to use the service if they cannot agree on a private arrangement.

How long an application takes

It can take up to 6 weeks to process an application and arrange a payment plan. Complex cases may take longer.

It will take longer to process if your child is not registered for Child benefit (opens in a new tab).
