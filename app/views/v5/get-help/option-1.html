A private arrangement is an agreement between parents. It usually includes a care arrangement and a financial arrangement. These describe how to manage your child's time with each parent and how to pay for their needs.

A private arrangement might be a good option if:

  • you and the the other parent can communicate about your child and situations that can affect how you provide for them
  • you feel safe dealing with the other parent
  • you need flexibility for payments
  • you both can follow the arrangement

You will need to manage care and finances yourself. You may decide to use third party support or advice.

Arrangements can be informal, in writing or throug a court order.

The Child Maintenance Service cannot influence or enforce a private arrangement.
