What is a private arrangement

Use this service to help you make decisions about your child’s care, finances or wellbeing, including if:

  • you are still in a relationship with your child's other parent
  • you do not live with the other parent
  • you are separating or have recently separated
  • the other parent is not involved or not known
We will not save your personal information or help manage private arrangements.
