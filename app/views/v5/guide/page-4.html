Making a care arrangement

There are many reasons why your child may split their time between parents. A care arrangement is an agreement between parents about how your child will split their time, including considerations like:

  • Where they spend the night
  • Who takes them to school and picks them up
  • When and where the child can see the parent they do not live with

You can:

  • get help making child arrangements if you divorce or separate, including a written plan, mediation or a court order
  • get support if you are separated from your children
