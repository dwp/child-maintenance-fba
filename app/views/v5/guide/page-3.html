If you cannot use a private arrangement

The Child Maintenance Service manages payments and can take action to recover missed payments. It may be a good choice if you:

  • cannot talk about finances with the other parent
  • want a service to manage payments for you
  • want an online account to keep track of your payments
  • have concerns for your safety or your child's safety
  • do not know where the other parent is or cannot contact them

Learn more about the Child Maintenance Service.
