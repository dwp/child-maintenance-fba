Using a private arrangement

The steps are recommended but you can do them in an order that fits your circumstances.

1. Work out the cost of your child

To help you organise and compare the costs of raising your child, we will ask how much you spend on everyday items and one-time costs for them.

You will be able to download the finished worksheet and can use the costs as part of the written agreement template.

We will not ask for any personal information and your answers will not be saved. All answers are optional.

2. Plan your conversation

Use this form to help you plan a conversation about child maintenance with your child's other parent. You can decide to share it with them or use it in mediation.

{{ govukButton({ text: "Start now", href: "../../v4/plan-conversation/start", isStartButton: true }) }}

3. Work out a payment amount

You can decide the payment amounts, what they include, and if they should be scheduled. When deciding payment amounts, parents often consider:

  • one or both parents' income
  • the child's costs
  • household expenses
  • other children they are responsible for
  • care arrangements

You may decide to use the calculator from the Child Maintenance Service. However, it only estimates your child maintenance payments. It cannot tell you exactly what you would get or pay. The Child Maintenance Service calculation considers:

  • the paying parent's income
  • other children the paying parent is responsible for
  • care arrangements

4. Create a financial agreement

Most children benefit from having both parents or guardians involved in their lives in a positive way, whether they live with them or not. Parents can help working together for their children's long-term benefit.

A financial agreement can help both of you to focus on supporting your children. Use it to make a record of what you have both agreed.

This form will ask you about:

  • your names and your children's names (optional)
  • any regular payments or financial support
  • any additional details you want to include

You do not need to include everything. Only add details that you both agree on.

You can download or print the finished form.

We will not require information that can identify you or save your answers.
