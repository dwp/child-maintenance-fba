Single parent support hub

This site is for anyone who is making decisions about their child's financial support, care or well-being.

I need to make a financial arrangement for my child

Use our tools to plan and manage financial support for your child, or explore the Child Maintenance Service if you cannot reach an agreement.

I need to review my financial arrangement

It is important to review your financial arrangement regularly. Our tools can help you decide when to review it, then set reminders.

I need financial support and advice

Explore resources that can help you apply for benefits, set a budget and manage debt.

I need help separating or divorcing

Get guidance about separation and divorce, including making care arrangements for your children.

I need support in difficult conversations

Find guidance about talking with the other parent, supporting your children and using mediation.

I need support for mental health or safety

Charities and local organisations can help in difficult situations. You can also get counselling for yourself or your child.
