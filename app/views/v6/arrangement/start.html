Most children benefit from having both parents positively involved in their lives. A financial agreement can help you both to focus on supporting your children. This form will ask you about:

  • your names and your children's names (optional)
  • any regular payments or other financial support
  • any additional details you want to include

You do not need to answer all the questions.

You can download or print the finished form.

How to use the financial arrangement

If both parents have not agreed on a financial arrangement yet, you can fill out what you think it should be. Then, you can share it with the other parent as part of your conversations or mediation.

If you have already agreed on a financial arrangement, use those details. You can use the finished form for your records.

! Warning This financial arrangement is not legally binding and it should to be used only for your own reference. It will not be saved or used by the Child Maintenance Service.
