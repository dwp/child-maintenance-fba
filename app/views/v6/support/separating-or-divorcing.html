Making a care arrangement

A care arrangement is an agreement between parents about how your child will split their time, including considerations like:

  • where they spend the night
  • who takes them to school and picks them up
  • when and where the child can see the parent they do not live with

Get help making child arrangements if you divorce or separate (opens in a new tab), including a written plan, mediation or a court order.

Get support as a single parent

These organisations offer a wide range of resources to help single parents with employment, finances, housing, legal rights, parenting, and more. Their contact methods include a mix of face-to-face and online.

Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Services (CAFCASS)

CAFCASS promotes the welfare of children, advises family courts, and helps represent children.

Telephone: 0300 456 4000
Website: Cafcass - Children and Family Court Advisory and Support Service (opens in a new tab)

Both Parents Matter

Both Parents Matter supports and informs parents and others who are excluded from the lives of their children, or who seek to share in parenting. It also provides a telephone helpline, a web forum, and regional meetings.

Telephone: 0300 0300 363
Website: Both Parents Matter (opens in a new tab) Email: fnf@fnf.org.uk

Gingerbread's single parent helpline

Gingerbread's Single Parent Helpline provides free information about benefits, child maintenance, contact, debt, education, employment, and housing.

Telephone: 0845 658 5258
Website: Gingerbread (opens in a new tab)

One Parent Families Scotland (OPFS)

OPFS provides a free telephone helpline, family support services, and childcare. It can give advice on benefits, child maintenance, housing, legal rights, returning to work or education, and tax.

Telephone: 0808 801 0323
Website: One Parent Families Scotland (opens in a new tab) Email: info@opfs.org.uk

Family Lives (formally ParentlinePlus)

Family Lives provides a free telephone helpline, live chat online support, parenting courses, and workshops.

Telephone: 0808 800 2222
Website: Family Lives (opens in a new tab)

Parentline Scotland

Parentline Scotland is provided by the charity Children First. It provides a free telephone helpline and email service.

Telephone: 0800 028 2233
Website: Children First (opens in a new tab) Email: parentlinescotland@children1st.org.uk

Parenting NI (formerly Parents Advice Centre)

Parenting NI is for any family member worried about a behavioural, emotional or relationship difficulty. It provides a free telephone helpline and face-to-face support at its centres.

Telephone: 0808 8010 722
Website: Parenting NI (opens in a new tab) Email: belfast@pachelp.org

Making Arrangements for Your Children

These organisations can help if you are unable to see or contact your child:

Both Parents Matter

Both Parents Matter supports and informs parents and others who are excluded from the lives of their children, or who seek to share in parenting. It also provides a telephone helpline, a web forum, and regional meetings.

Telephone: 0300 0300 363
Website: Both Parents Matter (opens in a new tab) Email: fnf@fnf.org.uk

MATCH - Mothers Apart From Their Children

MATCH offers non-judgemental emotional support to mothers who are separated from their children. However, you must have membership to access more personalised support.

Telephone: 0800 689 4104
Website: Match Mothers (opens in a new tab) Email: enquiries@matchmothers.org

The Centre for Separated Families

The Centre for Separated Families help parents, grandparents and other significant adults to find ways of building co-operative, post-separation parenting relationships that put the needs of children first. It provides an email advice service.

Telephone: 0845 478 6360
Website: Separated Families (opens in a new tab)
