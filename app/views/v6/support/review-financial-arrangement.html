There are many valid reasons to review the financial and care arrangments for your child. You can review your arrangement on your own or with the other parent.

There are two common ways to review your arrangement:

  • on a regular schedule
  • due to a change in your circumstances

Reviewing on a schedule

You can set review reminders with a certain frequency, for example every one or two years.

You can also choose to review on a certain date, for example on your child's birthday or the first day of school each year.

Reviewing for a change in your circumstances

It can be helpful to review your arrangement when there is:

  • an update to your care arrangment
  • an increase or decrease in income
  • a change in your benefits
  • a new medical condition
  • a milestone for your child (for example, starting a new school)
  • a major life change (for example, moving far away)
  • a new adoptive parent
  • a loss of a step-parent

There may be other reasons. You should review your arrangement when you feel it is appropriate.
