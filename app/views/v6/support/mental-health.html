Tell someone about the behaviours you or your children have experienced

Domestic abuse or violence is a crime. It is important to remember that there is help and support available to you, including police response, online support, refuges and other services.

Call any of the following organisations to get help and advice about domestic abuse.

! Warning Call 999 if it's an emergency or you're in immediate danger.
