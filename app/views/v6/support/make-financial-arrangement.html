A private financial arrangement is an agreement between parents about how to support their child, without involving the Child Maintenance Service.

When making a financial arrangment parents usually consider how much they spend for their child and regular payments from one parent to the other.

Work out how much you spend for your child

Use our worksheet to help you organise and compare how much you spend for your child, we will ask how much you spend on everyday items and one-time costs for them.

You will be able to download the finished worksheet and can use the costs to inform your financial arrangement.

Work out a payment amount

If you want to use regular payments, you can decide the amounts and schedule yourself or use the calculator from the Child Maintenance Service (opens in a new tab).

When deciding payment amounts, parents often consider:

  • one or both parents' income
  • spending for their children or household
  • other children they are responsible for
  • care arrangements

The official calculator estimates child maintenance payments. It considers:

  • the paying parent's income
  • other children the paying parent is responsible for
  • care arrangements

Create a financial arrangement

Create a financial arrangement to make a written record of what you propose to the other parent or agree with them.

This form will ask you about:

  • your names and your children's names (optional)
  • any regular payments or other financial support
  • any additional details you want to include

You can download or print the finished form. We will not save your answers.

Use the Child Maintenance Service

Use the government's Child Maintenance Service if you and the other parent cannot agree on a financial arrangement or if you feel it would be a safer option.

With the service, you can:

  • Get help arranging child maintenance (opens in a new tab)
  • Manage your child maintenance case online (opens in a new tab)
  • Contact the Child Maintenance Service (opens in a new tab)
