Learn how to tell your children about separation

The wellbeing of your child should be the highest priority during separation or divorce. There are many organisations that can help you talk to your child and support them.

Gingerbread

The Single Parent Helpline provides free information about benefits, child maintenance, contact, debt, education, employment, and housing.

Telephone: 0808 802 0925
Website: Gingerbread (opens in a new tab)

Family Lives (formally ParentlinePlus)

Family Lives provides a free telephone helpline, live chat online support, parenting courses, and workshops.

Telephone: 0808 800 2222
Website: Family Lives (opens in a new tab)

Parentline Scotland

Parentline Scotland is provided by the charity Children First. It provides a free telephone helpline and email service.

Telephone: 0800 028 2233
Website: Children First (opens in a new tab)
Email: parentlinescotland@children1st.org.uk

Tavistock

Tavistock provides face-to-face and online therapy, including counselling and psychotherapy for challenges in parenting, relationships, and sexual lives. Cost depends on choice of service.

Telephone: 0207 380 1960
Website: Tavistock relationships (opens in a new tab)

Find mediation resources

These organisations can help resolve conflicts between you and your child's other parent.

Relationships Scotland

A network of affiliated local services providing relationship counselling, family mediation, child contact centres and other family support services.

Telephone: 0345 119 2020
Website: Relationships Scotland (opens in a new tab) Email: enquiries@relationship-scotland.org.uk

Family Lives (formally ParentlinePlus)

National Family Mediation is an umbrella organisation for local, family mediation providers. It can provide a pack of 60 affiliated services. There are fees on sliding scale, including some public funding for low earners.

Telephone: 0300 100 1234
Website: Relate (opens in a new tab)

College of Mediators

The college is a membership body for mediators. It has a directory of mediators in England, Wales and Scotland. It includes privately run and not-for-profit mediation services.

Telephone: 0845 658 5258
Website: College of Mediators (opens in a new tab)
Email: admin@collegeofmediators.co.uk
