To help you organise and compare the costs of raising your child, we will ask how much you spend on everyday items and one-time costs for them. It may be easiest to use the amount you have spent in the past year.

The amount can be for one or both parents.

You will be able to download the finished worksheet and use the costs to create a financial arrangement (opens in a new tab)

We will not save your answers. They are optional and you can use estimated amounts.
