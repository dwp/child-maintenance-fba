Use this hub to find tools and guidance to help you support your child.

This hub is for anyone making decisions about their child’s care, finances or wellbeing, including if:

  • you do not live with the other parent
  • you are separating or have recently separated
  • the other parent is not involved or not known

We do not collect personal information or help with private arrangements.
