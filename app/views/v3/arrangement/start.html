Most children benefit from having both parents or guardians involved in their lives in a positive way, whether they live with them or not.

A financial arrangement can help both of you focus on supporting your children. Use it to record what you have agreed.

This form will ask you about:

  • Your names and your children's names
  • Any regular payments or financial support
  • Any additional details you want to include

You do not need to answer all the questions. You can complete this form together or separately, but you should only include details you both agree on.

You can download or print the finished form. You can also create an account to save it online.

! Warning This financial arrangement is not legally binding and it is intended to be used only for your own reference.
