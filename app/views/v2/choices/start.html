This service can be used help you understand your options for supporting your child. You can use the service together as parents or as an individual.

If you are not sure which tools you need, this service can help you decide what to do next. It will ask about any current financial and care arrangements, how you communicate as parents, and if you have concerns for your child's well being. It will then highlight which of our tools may be the most useful for you.

The service is confidential. You do not have to explain your situation and your answers will not be saved.
