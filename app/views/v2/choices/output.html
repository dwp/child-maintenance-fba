Based on the answers you gave us, we recommend certain tools for your situation, but what you do next is your decision.

You can use our resources to work out how to pay for your child's needs and get support for yourself.

Because you told us you are setting up a financial arrangement

Many parents prefer to have their agreements in writing. You can record decisions about who pays for your child's different needs, schedules and any extra considerations. We will build a document that you an download and sign, but it is not legally binding.

Use the financial arrangement tool (opens in a new tab)

Because you told us you want to work out costs

Our cost worksheet can help you organise and add up how much you spend on your child's needs. All costs are optional and we will not save your answers. You can take the cost worksheet to discussions to help agree on a financial arrangement.

Fill out the cost worksheet (opens in a new tab)

Because you told us you need to change or set up care arrangements

You can create a care arrangement plan that sets out where your child spends their time, including schedules, handovers, and holidays. It also covers additional topics such as religious practices, diet and who else has access. While the plan is not legally binding, support is available if you need to take further steps such as setting up court orders.

Use the Ministry of Justice service (opens in a new tab)
