These organisations can help if you are unable to see or contact your child.

Both Parents Matter

Both Parents Matter supports and informs parents and others who are excluded from the lives of their children, or who seek to share in parenting. It also provides a telephone helpline, a web forum, and regional meetings.

MATCH – Mothers Apart From Their Children

MATCH offers non-judgemental emotional support to mothers who are separated from their children. However, you must have membership to access more personalised support.

Website

The Centre for Separated Families

The Centre for Separated Families help parents, grandparents and other significant adults to find ways of building co-operative, post-separation parenting relationships that put the needs of children first. It provides an email advice service.
